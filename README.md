## 🎙️ Welcome to the 1940s Timeline

> *"Step into the 1940s. Experience the golden age of deep, cinematic postwar vintage roleplay in the heart of Sri Lanka."*

Goldstone County is live in the **1940s timeline era**. Classic automobiles, vintage radio airwaves, mobsters, private detectives, sheriffs, and authentic Americana come together in a living world.

The **Goldstone County Launcher** is an ultra-fast, zero-dependency standalone application crafted specifically for our community. Designed to eliminate connection headaches and technical hurdles, it synchronizes your RedM game engine and TeamSpeak 3 SaltyChat in one click, provides emergency one-click game repair tools, ReShade graphics enhancement, and plays atmospheric stream-safe music while you wait to enter the territory.

Everything is compiled into a **single, portable executable** (`Goldstone County.exe`) — no installers, no setup wizards, and no third-party runtimes required. Simply download and enter the 1940s!

- **Smooth Performance**: Native rendering hook with zero performance drops.

One-click setup for voice chat with live progress display.

- **Live Status & Progress**: Direct console logging showing download percentages and installation stages in the UI.
- **Zero Configuration**: Sets up all required audio profiles and plugins automatically.

### 🎵 4. Official Songs & Music Player

- **Instant Offline Playback**: Automatically caches songs to your local storage so your playlist loads instantly even without internet.

## 📥 Quick Start Guide

Getting started takes less than a minute:

1. **Download the Launcher**:
   Grab the latest [`Goldstone County.exe`](https://github.com/nimnabrox/goldstonelauncher/releases/latest/download/Goldstone.County.exe) (or [`goldstone-launcher.exe`](https://github.com/nimnabrox/goldstonelauncher/releases/latest/download/goldstone-launcher.exe)) from official releases.
2. **Place It Anywhere**:
   Drop `Goldstone County.exe` anywhere you like (e.g., Desktop or `C:\Games\Goldstone\`).
3. **Launch**:
   Double-click `Goldstone County.exe`.
4. **Enter Your Voice Callsign**:
   Type your registered TeamSpeak character name into the **TeamSpeak Username** field.
5. **Enter Goldstone County**:
   Click **ENTER GOLDSTONE (AUTO)**. The launcher will automatically boot TeamSpeak, connect to the voice server, and launch RedM directly into Goldstone County!
