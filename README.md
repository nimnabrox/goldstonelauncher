## ⚡ Key Features (v0.3.4 Winter Update)

Jump into Goldstone County quickly and easily with our built-in recovery tools and synchronization suite:

### 🚀 1. One-Click Dual-Sync
Opens **RedM** and **TeamSpeak 3** simultaneously, synchronizes your in-game RP voice callsign, routes your audio to the SaltyChat radio frequency, and connects you straight into Goldstone County (`cfx.re/join/qe3bl6`). Dedicated **Only RedM** and **Only TeamSpeak** modes are also available.

### 🎄 2. Winter Atmosphere & Live Server Status *(New in v0.3.4)*
Immerse yourself in the season from the moment the launcher opens:
- **Winter Cabin & Snowfall**: Embedded festive cabin background video, pine canopy header, and gentle foreground snowfall.
- **Merry Christmas Welcome**: Warm holiday greeting overlay plus a live Goldstone County server player counter right in the dashboard.
- **Christmas Portal Experience**: Matching animated night sky, moon, flying sleigh, countdown, and interactive vinyl player on [gscl.nimnabro.com](https://gscl.nimnabro.com/).

### 🎨 3. Goldstone ReShade Graphics
Enhance your Red Dead Redemption 2 visuals with custom 1940s vintage shaders.
- **One-Click ReShade Setup**: Automatically downloads and installs ReShade64 with pre-tuned vintage color grading.
- **In-Game Toggle**: Easily toggle ReShade on or off in-game anytime using your preferred hotkey (customizable in Launcher Settings).
- **Smooth Performance**: Native rendering hook with zero performance drops.

### 🎧 4. TS3 & SaltyChat Easy Installer
One-click setup for voice chat with live progress display.
- **Automatic Setup**: Automatically downloads and installs TeamSpeak 3 Client 64-bit and the SaltyChat plugin if missing.
- **Live Status & Progress**: Direct console logging showing download percentages and installation stages in the UI.
- **Zero Configuration**: Sets up all required audio profiles and plugins automatically.

### 🎵 5. Official Songs & Music Player *(Updated in v0.3.4)*
Atmospheric soundtrack automatically plays while waiting in queue or during loading screens, with smart auto-pause at the 67% loading screen.
- **New Featured Track**: Opens with *Goldstone - Christmas Remix 01* at the top of the playlist, alongside *Music Video (KRJ & EMD)*.
- **Official Community Catalog (13 Tracks)**: Includes *Christmas Remix 01*, *Our Journey*, *Halloween Special*, 6 *Horizen Remixes*, *Horizen Aurudu 2026*, and classic ambience.
- **Live Non-Interrupting Sync**: New catalog updates refresh cleanly in the background without interrupting playback.
- **Instant Offline Playback**: Automatically caches songs to your local storage so your playlist loads instantly even without internet.
- **Continuous Loop & Repeat Modes**: Loop individual songs or playback the complete album, or pick your own local MP3 folder anytime.

### ⚡ 6. Smart 67% Stuck Fix *(Improved in v0.3.4)*
Solves the notorious RedM 67% loading screen freeze. Launches clean into the game engine first, prepares memory allocations, and auto-connects to the server the moment assets are ready — now with a reliable auto-connect fallback timer for **Only RedM** launches too.

### ✨ 7. Vulkan & Cache Cleaner
One-click instant repair for graphical glitches, missing building textures, and RedM Vulkan shader initialization crashes without needing to restart your computer.

### 🛡️ 8. Process Deadlock Killer
If RedM, TeamSpeak, or Red Dead Redemption 2 freezes in the background after a crash, the **Force Close** tool immediately clears dead tasks from system memory so you can reconnect without opening Task Manager.

### 💾 9. Multi-Drive Auto-Detection
Automatically scans all local storage drives (SSDs, NVMe, HDDs) and common directories to locate your TeamSpeak and RedM installations without manual path configuration.

## 📥 Quick Start Guide

Getting started takes less than a minute:

1. **Download the Launcher**:
   Grab the latest [`Goldstone.County.exe`](https://github.com/nimnabrox/goldstonelauncher/releases/latest/download/Goldstone.County.exe) from official releases.
2. **Place It Anywhere**:
   Drop `Goldstone.County.exe` anywhere you like (e.g., Desktop or `C:\Games\Goldstone\`).
3. **Launch**:
   Double-click `Goldstone.County.exe`.
4. **Enter Your Voice Callsign**:
   Type your registered TeamSpeak character name into the **TeamSpeak Username** field.
5. **Enter Goldstone County**:
   Click **ENTER GOLDSTONE (AUTO)**. The launcher will automatically boot TeamSpeak, connect to the voice server, and launch RedM directly into Goldstone County!
